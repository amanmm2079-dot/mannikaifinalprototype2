# Mannik AI

### AI-Powered Mental Health Monitoring & Early Distress Detection

Mannik AI is an AI-powered mental health support and monitoring platform designed to help identify signs of psychological distress at an early stage and connect individuals with appropriate support.

The platform focuses on continuous mental-health check-ins, AI-assisted distress analysis, early alerts, and access to mental-health resources.

---

## 🚨 Problem

People experiencing trauma, social distress, violence, or prolonged legal and social difficulties may develop symptoms of anxiety, depression, PTSD, and other forms of psychological distress.

However, signs of worsening mental health may remain unnoticed until the situation becomes serious.

Mannik AI aims to provide an early-support system by continuously monitoring mental-health indicators and identifying potential distress patterns.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Mannik AI provides a simple and accessible digital platform that:

* Enables regular mental-health check-ins
* Uses AI-assisted analysis to identify potential distress
* Provides early alerts when concerning patterns are detected
* Helps connect users with appropriate support resources
* Provides a simple interface designed for accessibility
* Supports caseworker-assisted intervention where required

The system is designed as an **AI-assisted decision-support platform**, where important interventions remain under human supervision.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI-Assisted Distress Detection

Analyzes user responses and mental-health indicators to identify potential signs of psychological distress.

### 📊 Mental Health Monitoring

Allows continuous monitoring through regular check-ins and recorded responses.

### 🚨 Early Alerts

Potentially concerning patterns can generate alerts for authorized caseworkers or responsible personnel.

### 👨‍💼 Human-in-the-Loop

AI provides signals and recommendations, while authorized human personnel make the final decisions regarding intervention.

### 📱 Accessible Interface

Simple and intuitive interface designed to make the platform easier to use, including for users with limited digital literacy.

### 📚 Mental Health Resources

Provides access to relevant mental-health information and support resources.

### 🔐 Privacy & Security

Sensitive user information is intended to be protected through controlled access and secure data handling.

## 🔐 Privacy & Safety

Mannik AI is designed with privacy and responsible AI principles in mind.

Key principles include:

* Controlled access to sensitive information
* Human supervision for important decisions
* Minimization of unnecessary personal data
* Secure handling of user information
* AI outputs treated as assistance rather than final medical decisions

---

## 🎯 Target Users

The platform is designed primarily for:

* Individuals experiencing psychological distress
* Victims or survivors of traumatic experiences
* Authorized caseworkers
* Support organizations
* Relevant government or social-welfare personnel

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Mannik AI is a prototype intended to demonstrate an AI-assisted mental-health monitoring and support concept.

It is **not a replacement for a qualified mental-health professional, emergency service, or medical diagnosis**.

AI-generated assessments should be reviewed by appropriately authorized human personnel before significant action is taken.
